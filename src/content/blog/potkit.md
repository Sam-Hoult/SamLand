Next up from [VIVIDconcept](https://vividconcept.co), my product design house, is the [**PotKit**](https://vividconcept.co) - a smell-proof joint protector and lighter carrier designed for one-hand access on-the-go.

Like the [ROLLUX](https://vividconcept.co/rollux), the PotKit comes out of obsessing over a small, real, everyday annoyance: carrying joints around without crushing them, without the smell, and without a bulky case. Two sealed compartments let you keep your joints separate from your roaches (or mints), and a snug carrier slot holds a standard BIC lighter so everything you need is in one pocket-sized package. Joints protected. Ready to light.

It's **coming soon** - true to the VIVIDconcept motto, *form follows obsession*.

## Features
- 🛡️ **Joint protection** - Keeps joints from getting crushed in your pocket.
- 🚫 **Smell-proof storage** - Two sealed compartments to keep things contained.
- ✋ **One-hand access** - Designed to open and use easily with one hand.
- 🔥 **Heat-resistant material** - High-quality material chosen for heat resistance.
- 🗂️ **Two compartments** - Use one for joints and the other for roaches, mints, or anything small.
- 🔦 **Fits a standard BIC lighter** - A snug carrier slot for a classic everyday lighter (lighter not included).
- 👖 **Pocket-sized** - Compact profile designed for daily carry.
- 🔢 **Holds up to 6 joints** - Capacity for a full day (or weekend) out.

Built for discreet carry, quick access, and heat-resistant durability - without the bulk. Designed and made in Canada.
